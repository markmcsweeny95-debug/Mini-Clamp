# Mini-Clamp

Ultra Mini Desk Clamp 

Assembly instructions 

Collect all printed parts together on a table and locate the largest piece (a u shaped piece), label this piece part 1 
Then find the longest and chunkiest of the 2 screws and label this part 2 
Place part 2 through the hole in the end of part 1 and screw it in until it’s securely in
Now find the 2 parts which are the same which look like little anvils and call them part 3 and part 4 - slide the bottom section of part 3 into the groove on the opposite  side of the u shaped clamp to the other hole. Make sure the ridged side is facing out of the groove.
Then slide in piece 4 in the same way but make sure that the ridged side is facing into the other piece (into the groove)
Then find the final screw piece and screw it into the groove you just slid part 3 and 4 into and when you screw it in part 4 should move slowly closer to part 3 while still leaving a small gap so it doesn’t get stuck 
How to use
Use the larger screw in the main frame of the clamp to secure the clamp to the table tightly preventing movement ]
Use the smaller adjustable clamp on the top of the clamp to hold a small component or pcb to solder 
Tolerances 
The parts have a tight fit to ensure strength and security so depending on printers some or all parts may require additional rough sanding after printing in order to work well
Other notes:
To create more leverage on the main or top clamp you can place a shaft through the holes at the end of the clamp and turn slowly to provide further force.
